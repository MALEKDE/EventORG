    Our Mission

    Simplifying University Event Management

    We believe every campus event deserves seamless organization — from the first idea to the final applause. Najah Events replaces paperwork, phone calls, and waiting rooms with an elegant digital workflow.

    Our platform is built specifically for the unique needs of An-Najah National University — respecting the culture, scale, and ambitions of Palestine's largest university.

    Our Story

    From Idea to Platform

    What started as a frustration with manual booking forms evolved into the university's primary event management solution.

    Early 2024
    The Problem Identified
    Students and clubs spent weeks navigating manual booking forms and email chains just to reserve a room for a 2-hour meeting.
    Mid 2024
    Platform Conceptualized
    A team of CS students proposed a centralized digital solution — one dashboard for events, bookings, and approvals.
    Late 2024
    Beta Launch
    First version launched with basic venue booking and event browsing. 200+ students signed up in the first week.
    2025
    Full Feature Release
    Company portal, clubs module, admin dashboard, and real-time notifications added. 5,000+ active users.
    2026 →
    The Future
    Mobile apps, AI-powered event recommendations, and integration with the university's student portal on the roadmap.
